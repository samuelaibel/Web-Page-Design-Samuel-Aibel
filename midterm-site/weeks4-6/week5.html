Game Recap

A wild comeback ended in the NFL's first tie of the season. Down 27-13 early in the fourth quarter, the Giants scored two touchdowns to even it, with Malik Nabers catching the tying 17-yard score with 1:48 left. Jameis Winston threw for 326 yards and three touchdowns, and the defense sacked Jayden Daniels five times. Neither team scored in overtime, and three missed Giants field goals and a Cam Skattebo fumble loomed large.
