Game Recap

The Giants fell behind 14-3 in the first quarter, then took over. Jameis Winston threw three touchdown passes, Malik Nabers topped 100 receiving yards for the second time this season, and the defense intercepted Jacoby Brissett three times, the last of which sealed it: a 98-yard pick six by Deonte Banks as time expired.
