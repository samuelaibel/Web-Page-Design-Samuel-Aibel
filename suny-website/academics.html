Academics

Discover a wide range of academic programs designed to challenge students, encourage curiosity, and prepare graduates for their futures.

Explore Your Academic Interests

SUNY New Paltz offers undergraduate and graduate programs across the arts, humanities, sciences, business, education, and professional fields. Students are encouraged to explore different disciplines while developing the skills necessary for careers, graduate school, and lifelong learning.

The Academic Experience

Small-Class Environment

Students have opportunities to work closely with professors and participate actively in classroom discussions, projects, and collaborative learning.

Research & Discovery

Students can pursue research and independent projects that allow them to explore subjects beyond the traditional classroom experience.

Interdisciplinary Learning

Students can explore connections between different academic disciplines and build an education tailored to their interests.

Preparing for the Future

Academic programs help students develop writing, research, communication, analytical, and problem-solving skills that can be applied to careers and graduate study.
