About SUNY New Paltz

The State University of New York at New Paltz is a public university located in New Paltz, New York. Founded in 1828, the university has developed into a comprehensive institution offering undergraduate and graduate programs across a wide range of disciplines.


The campus combines a traditional college environment with access to the natural beauty, history, and culture of the Hudson Valley. Students can take advantage of academic opportunities, student organizations, athletics, and the surrounding community.

Academics

Humanities

Explore history, philosophy, languages, literature, political science, and other fields that examine society and human culture.

Sciences

Study subjects including biology, chemistry, mathematics, computer science, and environmental science.

Business & Economics

Develop knowledge of economics, business, finance, management, and related areas of study.

Campus Life

Life Beyond the Classroom

SUNY New Paltz offers students numerous opportunities to get involved outside of their classes.

Students can participate in clubs and organizations, intramural sports, athletics, performing arts, student government, and a variety of campus events.

The surrounding Hudson Valley also provides opportunities for hiking, biking, outdoor recreation, and exploring nearby towns and cultural attractions.

New Paltz, New York

SUNY New Paltz is located in the Hudson Valley, approximately 75 miles north of New York City and close to the Shawangunk Ridge.

The location gives students access to both a college-town atmosphere and the natural landscapes of the Hudson Valley.
